## 👨‍💻 About me

I'm an AI engineer with a **Master's degree in Artificial Intelligence and Information Processing** from Badji Mokhtar University, Annaba. I build models and tools that turn raw data into something useful, from machine-learning pipelines to full-stack web apps.

My master's thesis applied **unsupervised learning** to predict COVID-19 vaccine side effects, and I turned it into a working web application used with real healthcare data during an internship at **EPSP Annaba**.

Alongside engineering, I've worked as a **freelance video editor since 2021** with 200+ completed projects for clients worldwide. It taught me to think in timelines, pacing and delivery, and I bring the same discipline to my technical work.
